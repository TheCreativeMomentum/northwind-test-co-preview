FAQ

Questions we actually get

How long does it take to get parts?

If it's in stock, we stage it the same day we confirm the order. If it isn't, we give you a real lead time before you commit — not after.

Do you actually keep stock, or is that marketing?

We stock the parts mid-market operators burn through: motors, seals, controls, wear items. We'll tell you what's on the shelf and what has to be sourced. No mystery inventory.

Who do I talk to after I send the request?

One accountable contact. You get a name on the first reply. That person owns the job until it is closed.

How do demos work?

Book a demo and we walk the homepage flow and the intake: what you send, how we stage, who you talk to. Thirty minutes, no slide deck marathon. Use the form or the Book a demo button.
